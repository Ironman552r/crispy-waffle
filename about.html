About NXG

Development
by Design.

NXG Tennis Academy builds a clear, personal pathway for every player in Anna and Melissa, Texas.

Where you start doesn't define where you can go.

Every player deserves a development plan. That's why we don't simply place players based on age. We consider their experience, skill, movement, competitive readiness, and developmental needs to determine the training environment that makes the most sense for them.

Every athlete begins with an on-court trial and evaluation. Our coaches assess your player's current abilities, learn about their goals, and recommend the training group that best fits their development.
