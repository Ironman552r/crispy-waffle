Already have questions?

Is the trial really free?

Yes. Every player starts with a free on-court trial and evaluation before joining a program.

My player has never played. Is that okay?

Absolutely. NXG works with players from first-time red ball beginners to high-performance juniors.

How do you decide which group my player joins?

We look at experience, skill, movement, competitive readiness and developmental needs, not just age.

Which location will the trial be at?

You'll choose your preferred location (Anna or Melissa) in the questionnaire, and we'll do our best to match it.
